## 1. Purpose

This plan tells employees how to report and handle security incidents.

## 2. Scope

The plan applies to all employees and contractors.

## 2.1 Locations

Main office and remote work locations.

## 3. Reporting a Suspected Incident

Send an email to the security mailbox with this information:

1. Name of the person who reports the incident
2. Contact information
3. Description of the incident

## 4. Severity Levels

The incident response team assigns a severity level.

## 4.1 Severity Table

Low: 30 days. Medium: 14 days. High: 7 days.
